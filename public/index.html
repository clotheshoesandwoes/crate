crate

make it yours

crate reads your Spotify library to learn your taste, then digs through open music graphs — not Spotify's promo machine — for things you haven't heard. Finds save back to your Liked Songs and a playlist. Everything stays in your browser: your library, your finds, your login. Nothing is shared.

Spotify caps shared apps at 5 users, so each person brings their own free key — three minutes, one time, yours forever:

  1. open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your normal Spotify account
  2. Create app — name it anything. Redirect URI: http://127.0.0.1:8823/callback — add it, tick Web API, save
  3. copy the Client ID from the app's settings and paste it here

invited by the site owner? skip all of it — leave the field empty and just hit Connect Spotify.

No Spotify yet? Type an artist in the top bar and dig — previews come from open sources and work without an account. Everything — including your Spotify connection — stays on this device.
