crate

set up

crate reads your Spotify library to learn your taste, then digs through open music graphs — not Spotify's promo machine — for things you haven't heard. Finds save back to your Liked Songs and a Crate finds playlist.

  1. open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your normal Spotify account
  2. Create app — name it anything. Redirect URI: http://127.0.0.1:8823/callback — add it, tick Web API, save
  3. copy the Client ID from the app's settings and paste it here

No Spotify yet? Type an artist in the top bar and dig — previews come from open sources and work without an account. Everything is stored in data\store.json on this machine.
